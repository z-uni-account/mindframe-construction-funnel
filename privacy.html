Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 August 2026

This policy explains what Mindframe Media collects when you fill in a form on this website or on one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, what we do with it, and how to get it removed.

Mindframe Media is a trading name of Arreola Familia LLC ("Mindframe Media", "we", "us"). It applies to mindframeconstruction.com and to the instant lead forms we run on Facebook and Instagram.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you apply through our application form or through a Facebook or Instagram instant form, we collect:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your company name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Your answers to our qualifying questions: the type of construction work your company does, your approximate annual revenue band, the area you work in, and how soon you are looking to invest in marketing

On a Facebook or Instagram instant form, some of these fields are filled in automatically by Meta from your profile. You can edit any of them before you submit, and nothing reaches us until you do.

Information collected automatically. When you visit this website we also collect:

  • Advertising identifiers that tell us which ad you came from, held in the web address as UTM parameters and a Facebook click identifier
  • Standard technical information such as your browser type, device type, approximate location derived from your IP address, and the pages you viewed
  • Whether you played the video and how far through it you got

We do not collect payment details on this website, and we do not ask for social security numbers, government identification, or financial account information at any point in the application process.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your application — by phone, text or email, to talk about working together and to arrange a call.
  • To decide whether we are a fit. Your answers to the qualifying questions are used to work out whether we can actually help your company before we take up your time.
  • To measure and improve our advertising. We report conversions back to Meta so their system learns which ads reach the right companies. This is covered in the next section.
  • To send follow-up emails about the offer you applied for. Every one of them has an unsubscribe link, and replying to any of them stops the sequence.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with other contractors or agencies. We are not a shared-lead marketplace. Your details go to us and to the service providers listed below who operate our systems on our behalf, and nowhere else.

Advertising and measurement

This website uses the Meta pixel, and we also send matching events to Meta server-to-server through their Conversions API. This tells Meta when someone submits an application or books a call so their advertising system can find more companies like you. The information sent for this purpose is limited to the event itself, the advertising identifiers above, and a hashed (irreversibly scrambled) version of your email address and phone number. Meta's own handling of that data is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

You can limit this from Meta's side in your Facebook ad preferences, and most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their own settings.

Session analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how this page is used. It records how far people scroll, what they click, and how long they stay, so we can see where the page is confusing and fix it. Typed text is masked before it ever leaves your browser, and Clarity does not have access to the application form itself. Microsoft's handling of that data is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of established providers to run this. Each one only receives what it needs to do its job:

  • Meta Platforms — advertising delivery and instant lead forms
  • Tally — the application form on this website
  • Zapier — moving your application into our CRM
  • GoHighLevel (HighLevel Inc.) — our CRM, calendar and email sending
  • Vidalytics — video hosting and play-rate statistics
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymised page-usage statistics and session replay

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

How long we keep it

We keep application records in our CRM for as long as we may reasonably do business with you, and no longer than three years after your last contact with us, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or we are required to keep them longer.

Your choices

  • Stop the emails — use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any of them, or just reply and say so.
  • Stop the calls and texts — tell us on the phone, or reply STOP to a text.
  • See it, correct it, or delete it — email us at the address below and we will action it within 30 days.

If you live in California, the CCPA gives you the right to know what personal information we have collected about you, to have it deleted, to correct it, and not to be discriminated against for asking. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Use the email address below to exercise any of these rights; we will verify your identity by replying to the email address or phone number already on your record.

Children

This is a service sold to businesses. It is not directed at anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Your information is held in the systems listed above, which are access-controlled and encrypted in transit. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we limit access to the people who need it to speak with you.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
